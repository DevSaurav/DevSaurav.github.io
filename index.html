Vineyard data management platform

Full-stack lead
Team

Challenge

Give Australian vineyard growers one place to see weather, soil and crop-health data. The project is backed by Australian agricultural agencies, with support from Wine Australia.

The platform is live, with grower onboarding, role-based access and alerts in production.

05 · EB Pearls

Video dating platform

Role
Back-end development lead
Client
Sydney-based startup
Delivered
Feb 2024 · Android and iOS

Challenge

Match online users in real time and pair them for short video calls, at scale and at a sensible cloud cost.

What I built

  • A virtual lobby that pairs users for two-minute video calls, where they can like, rate and match
  • The core matching algorithm, which finds online users across about 30 preferences
  • NestJS microservices behind an Apollo Federation gateway, using RabbitMQ and TCP between services
  • Live user activity and availability over Socket.IO, phone verification with Twilio, and email with SES
  • In-app subscriptions that limit free users to three lobby sessions a day
  • CI/CD to AWS Fargate on ECS, managed directly with the client under a project manager

Outcome

Shipped to Android and iOS. Load tests were planned for 5,000 concurrent users, with tuned AWS cluster and Elasticsearch sizing to keep costs down.

NestJSApollo FederationRabbitMQMongoDBElasticsearchSocket.IOECS Fargate
